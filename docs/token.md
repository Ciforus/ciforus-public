# Token

CIFORUS is the ecosystem token of the Ciforus platform.

The product comes first. The token is designed to serve as the economic layer connected to the product ecosystem.

## Why the Token Exists

Ciforus is a privacy-first product ecosystem. The token exists to support the economic layer of that ecosystem.

The token is positioned for:

- subscription tier upgrades
- discounted payments in supported checkout flows
- staking-based access direction
- rewards and incentives
- Pay Link and payment support
- usage-linked burn logic
- treasury and liquidity support tied to eligible usage
- future ecosystem feature expansion

## Optional at Checkout, Important to the Ecosystem

CIFORUS is not positioned as mandatory for all platform access.

Ciforus can support other crypto-based payment assets such as USDT, USDC, ETH, and BNB.

However, CIFORUS is the native asset of the ecosystem. It is the token that connects access, discounts, rewards, burn logic, treasury support, and future utility expansion under one economic layer.

## Utility Direction

### Discounts

CIFORUS is positioned to provide an additional 20% discount when used in supported checkout flows.

### Tier Upgrades

The token is designed to support tier upgrades inside the Ciforus ecosystem.

### Staking-Based Access

A confirmed direction is staking-based tier activation as an alternate path to unlock higher plans. Exact thresholds and parameters should be announced only when finalized.

### Rewards

The token model includes ecosystem rewards and participation incentives.

### Pay Links and Payments

Pay Links and broader crypto-native payment flows give CIFORUS a natural role inside the product economy.

### Future Expansion

Future utility can expand into storage, messaging, identity, credits, premium features, and broader ecosystem actions.

## Usage-Linked Deflation

The token model is designed so eligible token usage can be divided as follows:

- 40% burn
- 40% treasury support
- 20% liquidity support

This connects token use to supply reduction, long-term operating support, and liquidity support.

This should not be interpreted as a guaranteed price outcome. It is a structural model tied to eligible platform usage.
