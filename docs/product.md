# Product

Ciforus is presented as a connected privacy ecosystem. The product direction combines communication, storage, identity, account protection, and crypto-native payment workflows in one environment.

## Product Thesis

Ciforus treats privacy as a system-level design choice.

The platform is not positioned as a single-purpose messenger, file drive, note app, or payment tool. It is designed around connected modules that reinforce one another.

## Core Value Pillars

The product is framed around four value pillars:

- private communication
- secure ownership
- premium identity
- calm workspace

## Modules

### 1. Email

Ciforus Email is positioned around private communication and premium digital identity.

Public product direction includes:

- `@ciforus.com` email identity
- Ciforus-to-Ciforus encrypted communication direction
- secure communication with external providers through standards-compatible transport security
- custom aliases based on tier
- disposable addresses based on tier
- future webmail maturity

Important boundary:

Ciforus-to-Ciforus email is where internal encryption guarantees apply most directly. External providers do not natively process Ciforus-specific encrypted payloads, so cross-provider delivery uses interoperable email standards and strong transport security rather than the same internal Ciforus-native guarantees.

### 2. Wallet Messaging

Ciforus Messaging is designed around wallet identity instead of username-based or phone-number-based discovery.

Public product direction includes:

- wallet addresses as a communication identity layer
- verified wallet ownership
- encrypted message handling
- pending encrypted delivery for not-yet-registered recipient wallets
- release of pending messages after wallet verification
- reduced need for public profile exposure

### 3. Secure Storage

Ciforus Storage is positioned as confidentiality-first encrypted storage for important files.

Public product direction includes:

- encrypted storage
- zero-knowledge file protection principles
- private ownership tied to Ciforus identity
- secure sharing between verified Ciforus users
- storage tiers aligned with product plans

The storage module is not positioned as mass media storage. It is designed for sensitive documents, records, and high-value files.

### 4. Notes

Ciforus Notes is an encrypted private writing environment.

Public product direction includes:

- encrypted notes
- labels and organization
- pinning
- private workspace control
- sensitive writing, drafts, records, and research

The notes module reflects the idea that private thinking is also sensitive data.

### 5. Wallet Identity

Wallet Identity is the bridge between crypto-native ownership and the Ciforus platform.

Public product direction includes:

- wallet ownership verification through cryptographic signatures
- wallet identity as a trust layer
- communication and interaction without unnecessary personal exposure
- tier-based wallet limits

Wallet identity is not framed as a broad public social directory. It is designed for controlled trust and private interaction.

### 6. Pay Links

Pay Links extend Ciforus into crypto-native payment requests.

Public product direction includes:

- shareable public payment request pages
- donation-style links
- invoice-style links
- recipient does not need a Ciforus account to complete payment
- direct wallet settlement direction
- owner notification after payment detection

Ciforus is not positioned as a custodial wallet for received Pay Link funds.

### 7. Security Center

The Security Center is the account protection and recovery surface of the product.

Public product direction includes:

- two-factor authentication
- recovery email setup and verification
- 12-word recovery phrase direction
- BIP39-based recovery reinforcement
- wallet verification methods
- session management and visibility
- account-level defense workflows

## Payment Philosophy

Ciforus frames payment privacy as part of the product thesis.

The platform direction supports crypto-native payment methods such as USDT, USDC, ETH, BNB, and CIFORUS. The purpose is to keep subscription and payment behavior aligned with the broader privacy-first model.

## Product Summary

Ciforus is built as a private digital environment where communication, storage, identity, notes, security, and payments are designed to operate under one product philosophy.

The platform is the foundation. The token is the ecosystem layer.
