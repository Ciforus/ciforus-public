# FAQ

## What is Ciforus?

Ciforus is a privacy-first, crypto-native digital platform built around secure communication, encrypted storage, wallet-aware identity, account protection, and payment workflows.

## Is this a development repository?

No.

This repository is a public information hub. It does not contain the private Ciforus application source code.

## Why does this repository exist?

It exists to organize public Ciforus information in a clear, reviewable format.

The goal is to help users, community members, technical readers, and skeptical evaluators understand the product, token, tokenomics, trust resources, and official links.

## What are the main product modules?

The public product direction includes:

- Email
- Wallet Messaging
- Secure Storage
- Notes
- Wallet Identity
- Pay Links
- Security Center

## Is Ciforus only a token project?

No.

Ciforus is positioned as a product-first privacy ecosystem. The token is the economic layer of the platform.

## What does the CIFORUS token do?

CIFORUS is positioned for:

- subscription discounts
- tier upgrades
- staking-based access direction
- rewards and incentives
- Pay Link and payment support
- usage-linked burn logic
- future ecosystem expansion

## Is the token mandatory for all users?

No.

Ciforus can support other crypto-based payment assets. CIFORUS is not positioned as mandatory for all access, but it is the native asset designed to coordinate ecosystem utility.

## What is the total supply?

100,000,000 CIFORUS.

## Is the token mintable?

No. The public token facts state that CIFORUS has a fixed supply with no minting and no inflation.

## How does the burn model work?

Under the current model, eligible CIFORUS-denominated platform usage is divided as:

- 40% burn
- 40% treasury support
- 20% liquidity support

This is intended to connect real usage to supply reduction and ecosystem support.

## Does the burn model guarantee token price appreciation?

No.

No public communication should claim guaranteed financial outcomes. Token price depends on many factors including adoption, liquidity, execution, market conditions, and demand.
