# Ciforus Presale Review Checklist

This draft is intended for the public Ciforus information repository.

## Purpose

Use this checklist to evaluate Ciforus through public verification surfaces before making any presale decision.

This is not investment advice. It is a structured review path.

## Product Context

- Review the main website: https://ciforus.com
- Check the token page: https://ciforus.com/token
- Confirm that the token is presented as the economic layer around the Ciforus privacy product.
- Review the product context around private email, encrypted notes, secure storage, wallet identity, wallet messaging, Pay Links, and account security controls.

## Token Utility Review

Review whether the token role is clear across:

- discounts
- access and tier upgrades
- rewards and incentives
- Pay Links and payment flows
- voluntary staking and product benefits
- external claiming of eligible available CIFORUS
- usage-linked burn mechanics

## Tokenomics Review

Check the public materials for:

- fixed supply
- no minting after deployment
- presale allocation
- ecosystem and rewards allocation
- treasury allocation
- team allocation
- liquidity allocation
- presale buyer ownership and claim behavior
- voluntary staking rules and separate team vesting
- presale stage pricing

## Risk Review

Any presale includes risk. Review:

- product adoption risk
- execution risk
- liquidity risk
- regulatory uncertainty
- token market risk
- sale participation risk
- team transparency considerations
- phishing and impersonation risk

## Final Review Frame

Ciforus should be reviewed as a product-backed utility token presale.

The important question is not only whether the token exists. The important question is whether the token is connected to a real product environment with public documents, deployed contract information, audit materials, defined tokenomics, and a clear utility path.
